# 📱 Meta Ads Performance Analysis Dashboard

## Project Title / Headline

**Meta Ads Performance Analysis Dashboard (Facebook & Instagram)**

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze advertising performance across Facebook and Instagram campaigns, providing insights into campaign effectiveness, audience engagement, conversions, demographics, geographic reach, and ad format performance.

---

## Purpose

The Meta Ads Performance Dashboard helps marketers evaluate campaign performance across the entire marketing funnel. From impressions and clicks to engagements, conversions, and purchases. The dashboard enables data-driven decision-making by identifying high-performing audiences, ad formats, and optimization opportunities.

---

## Data Source

**Source:** Meta Advertising Campaign Dataset

The dataset contains campaign-level advertising metrics including impressions, clicks, engagements, conversions, purchases, demographics, geographic reach, ad formats, and budget allocation across Facebook and Instagram campaigns.

---

## Dashboard Overview

### Business Problem

Digital advertising campaigns generate large volumes of performance data across platforms, audiences, and ad formats. Without a centralized reporting solution, marketers struggle to evaluate campaign effectiveness, optimize budgets, and improve conversion outcomes.

**Key Business Questions:**

* Which platform generates better campaign performance?
* Which audience segments drive the highest engagement?
* Which ad formats produce the strongest results?
* How does campaign performance vary across regions and time periods?
* Where are conversion opportunities being lost in the marketing funnel?

### Goal of the Dashboard

* Monitor campaign performance across Facebook and Instagram.
* Analyze user engagement and conversion behavior.
* Evaluate audience demographics and geographic reach.
* Compare advertising formats and campaign effectiveness.
* Support marketing optimization through data-driven insights.

### Key Visuals

* **Marketing Funnel Analysis** – Track performance from impressions to purchases.
* **Demographic Analysis** – Performance breakdown by age and gender.
* **Geographic Analysis** – Regional distribution of campaign reach.
* **Weekly Trend Analysis** – Campaign performance trends over time.
* **Hourly Performance Analysis** – Identify peak engagement periods throughout the day.
* **Calendar Heatmap** – Daily campaign activity and engagement patterns.
* **Ad Type Performance Analysis** – Comparison of Stories, Video, Image, and Carousel ads.
* **Platform Comparison** – Facebook vs Instagram campaign performance.
* **Interactive Tooltips** – Additional campaign insights displayed on hover for deeper analysis.

### Key Insights

* Facebook generated the highest overall reach, engagement, and purchase volume.
* Instagram achieved a slightly higher CTR (11.86%) and Engagement Rate (13.60%), indicating stronger audience interaction.
* Facebook delivered a higher Conversion Rate (5.21%) and Purchase Rate (0.61%), resulting in more purchases.
* The 18–30 age group contributed the highest share of campaign impressions.
* Female audiences represented the largest audience segment across both platforms.
* Video and Stories ads consistently delivered the strongest performance across engagement and conversion metrics.
* Strong awareness and engagement metrics indicate effective campaign targeting, while conversion metrics highlight opportunities for funnel optimization.
